# Dr. Erdinç Örsan ÜNAL

### Geomatics Engineer · GIS & Remote Sensing · Geospatial Software Developer

I develop geospatial software that connects engineering practice, spatial data and research. My work focuses on GIS, CAD interoperability, remote sensing and natural hazard assessment for energy infrastructure.

Based in Ankara, Türkiye, I bring more than 20 years of experience in pipeline projects, surveying, GIS and land acquisition. Through **Jeomatik**, I build practical tools for desktop, mobile and GIS workflows.

## Areas of Interest

- **Geospatial software:** desktop, web and mobile applications for spatial data.
- **GIS & CAD interoperability:** NCZ, DWG, DXF and DGN workflows.
- **Remote sensing & machine learning:** spatial analysis and environmental applications.
- **Pipeline safety:** GIS-based multi-hazard assessment and route planning.
- **Land management:** cadastral data, parcel management and land acquisition workflows.

## Selected Projects

### [Jeomatik NCZ Reader](https://github.com/erdincunal/Jeomatik-NCZ-Reader)
A QGIS plugin that imports Netcad NCZ files and converts supported entities into editable vector layers.

### Jeomatik Viewer
A mobile and desktop geospatial viewer for iOS and macOS, supporting NCZ, DWG, DXF and DGN files, map and satellite basemaps, snapping, measurement and data export.

### Geospatial Engineering Tools
Desktop tools and libraries for geometry processing, map interaction, CAD data workflows and cadastral parcel management.
